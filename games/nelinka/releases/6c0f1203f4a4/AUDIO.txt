Water impacts: assets/audio/splash-[1-3].wav
Source: "40 CC0 water / splash / slime SFX" by rubberduck, splash_02/03/08.ogg.
https://opengameart.org/content/40-cc0-water-splash-slime-sfx
https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/water-splash-slime-sfx.zip
License: CC0, as offered on the source page.
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Downloaded 2026-10-18. Foley is trimmed at the first transient, filtered, converted
to 22050 Hz mono 16-bit PCM, faded and gain-matched with a peak ceiling. Five
variants per walking material and three splashes avoid immediate repetition.
assets/audio/sources.json records the input and output SHA-256 hashes.
Foley is embedded in the native executable and WASM, with no runtime fetch.

Jump, spring, pickups, interactions, respawn and completion effects remain original
procedural synthesis in src/sound.rs, generated in memory at startup.
