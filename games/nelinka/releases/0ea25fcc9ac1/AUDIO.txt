Downloaded 2026-10-11. Edited to a single 0.4-second bark, filtered,
faded and gain-adjusted with peak limiting. This is a stock recording,
not a recording of Neli herself.

Additional gameplay effects are original procedural synthesis in src/sound.rs.
They are generated in memory at startup; no third-party sound samples are used
for footsteps, jumps, landings, pickups, interactions, splashes or completion.
